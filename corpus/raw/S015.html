Gamota Launches Vietnam Game Industry Monthly Report — May 2026

Jun 16, 2026
  • Vietnam mobile game downloads rebounded to 121M, while IAP revenue reached USD 23M
  • Midcore downloads softened, but Midcore Total Revenue continued to grow
  • The report highlights subgenre performance, new releases, localization signals, and industry updates

Gamota officially releases Vietnam Game Industry Monthly Report — May 2026. The report focuses on notable market movements in May 2026, including downloads, revenue, genre performance, new game launches, and key updates across the industry ecosystem.

In May, Vietnam’s mobile game market showed signs of recovery at the overall level, with total downloads rising to 121 million and IAP revenue reaching USD 23 million. However, the picture was not entirely even: midcore downloads declined, while Midcore Total Revenue continued to grow. This suggests that market value in May was not driven by traffic growth alone, but also by Live-Ops execution, stable player communities, and effective monetization loops.

Through these analyses, the May report helps publishers, developers, and international partners better understand the short-term signals shaping Vietnam’s game market.

Core Contents of the Report

The report combines market data and in-depth analysis, focusing on the following key areas:

  • Market downloads and revenue overview: Analyze download trends, IAP revenue, and the difference between overall market growth and midcore performance.
  • Subgenre performance and top mobile games: Update key subgenres by downloads and total revenue, including Battle Royale, MOBA, Sim Sports, Shooter, Sandbox, Idle RPG, Team Battle, and more.
  • Signals from new game releases: Explain why a high volume of new launches does not automatically lead to strong performance, while products with clear cultural fit, football affinity, or legacy gameplay memory showed stronger traction.
  • Industry ecosystem updates: Summarize notable developments in esports, IP partnerships, industry-facing activities, and new compliance requirements in Vietnam.

What Value Will You Gain?

This report is designed for publishers, developers, investors, and strategy teams in the game industry, offering practical value across several areas:

  • Track monthly market movements quickly: Follow the latest changes in downloads, revenue, subgenres, and top games in Vietnam.
  • Understand the real drivers of growth: Look beyond total downloads to better understand revenue sources, Live-Ops, player communities, and event-based monetization.
  • Evaluate genre and product opportunities: Identify content areas with notable traction, from long-running competitive subgenres to new products with familiar cultural elements.
  • See the role of localization more clearly: Understand how cultural fit, wuxia/xianxia themes, football affinity, familiar IP, and legacy gameplay memory can influence player attraction and revenue conversion.

Vietnam Game Industry Monthly Report — May 2026 is a reference resource for businesses seeking to better understand short-term movements in Vietnam’s game market and the signals that matter for future publishing strategies.
